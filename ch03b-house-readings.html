Section 03b

House-by-House Readingभाव-फल

House 1 · Self, body तनु

Chart: Gemini Lagna (Punarvasu); lord Mercury in the 3rd; aspected by Mars and Saturn

Youthful look, quick mind, talkative and adaptable. Mars and Saturn aspecting the Lagna add toughness and seriousness; life improves steadily with age.

युवा रूप, तेज़ बुद्धि, अनुकूलनशील; मंगल-शनि की दृष्टि से दृढ़ता व गंभीरता; उम्र के साथ जीवन बेहतर।

House 2 · Wealth, family, speech धन

Chart: Cancer; Venus + Rahu; lord Moon exalted in the 12th

Pleasant, persuasive speech; family is comfortable but values can clash; money comes through varied channels and is spent freely. Watch diet and throat.

मधुर वाणी; परिवार सुखी पर मूल्यों में मतभेद; विविध स्रोतों से धन, खुला खर्च।

House 3 · Courage, siblings, effort सहज

Chart: Leo; Sun + Mercury; lord Sun in own house

Courageous, self-made, good at writing, media, sales and short travel. Younger sibling likely and capable. Strong house.

साहसी, स्व-निर्मित; लेखन, मीडिया, बिक्री में कुशल; छोटे भाई/बहन सक्षम।

House 4 · Home, mother, property सुख

Chart: Virgo; empty; lord Mercury in the 3rd

Home life is practical; more than one change of residence. Mother is intelligent and busy. Property comes through own effort, best in Mercury periods.

घरेलू जीवन व्यावहारिक; एक से अधिक बार घर बदलना; संपत्ति स्वयं के प्रयास से।

House 5 · Children, intellect पुत्र

Chart: Libra; empty; lord Venus in the 2nd with Rahu; aspected by Jupiter and Saturn

Creative and analytical intelligence; romance plays a role before marriage. Children indicated with some delay; Jupiter's aspect protects them.

रचनात्मक बुद्धि; विवाह से पहले प्रेम-भावना; संतान कुछ विलंब से, गुरु की रक्षा।

House 6 · Enemies, debt, service, illness रिपु

Chart: Scorpio; Mars in own sign

Strongest house for winning: exams, competition, disputes, health recovery. Good for service-sector work. Loans are handled well.

प्रतियोगिता, परीक्षा, विवाद व रोग पर विजय; सेवा-क्षेत्र में सफलता।

House 7 · Spouse, partnership जाया

Chart: Sagittarius; empty; lord Jupiter in the 11th; aspected by Jupiter

Marriage is protected and stable. Spouse is principled, educated, mature, possibly older or from a different region; meeting through friends or social circles.

विवाह सुरक्षित व स्थिर; जीवनसाथी सिद्धांतवादी, शिक्षित, परिपक्व; मित्रों के माध्यम से परिचय।

House 8 · Longevity, hidden, sudden आयु

Chart: Capricorn; Ketu; lord Saturn in the 11th

Sudden events and research interest; in-laws are traditional and can feel distant; inheritance or insurance gains come late.

अचानक घटनाएँ, शोध में रुचि; ससुराल पारंपरिक; विरासत देर से।

House 9 · Fortune, dharma, father धर्म

Chart: Aquarius; empty; lord Saturn debilitated in the 11th; aspected by Mars

Luck improves with age; father works hard and faces early struggle; higher studies or long travel bring results after delays.

भाग्य उम्र के साथ बढ़ता; पिता परिश्रमी; उच्च शिक्षा देर से फलदायी।

House 10 · Career, status कर्म

Chart: Pisces; empty; lord Jupiter in the 11th

Career in organisations, advisory, finance, education, healthcare or IT; steady rise; strongest in Jupiter MD (2030–46).

संस्थागत करियर; स्थिर उन्नति; गुरु महादशा में सबसे मज़बूत।

House 11 · Gains, friends लाभ

Chart: Aries; Jupiter + Saturn

Excellent gains house: income grows steadily, influential friends, elder sibling support. Ambitions are fulfilled, mostly after 30.

उत्तम लाभ भाव: स्थिर आय-वृद्धि, प्रभावशाली मित्र; इच्छाएँ 30 के बाद पूरी।

House 12 · Loss, foreign, sleep व्यय

Chart: Taurus; exalted Moon

Comfortable bedroom life, good sleep when calm, foreign settlement or long stays abroad possible, spending on comfort and charity; spiritual depth.

सुखद शयन-सुख; विदेश-निवास संभव; सुविधा व दान पर खर्च; आध्यात्मिक गहराई।
