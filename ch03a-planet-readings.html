Section 03a

Planet-by-Planet Readingग्रह-फल

Sun सूर्य 3rd lord in the 3rd (moolatrikona), with Mercury

Sun in Leo, its moolatrikona, in the 3rd. Strong willpower and initiative; the native learns by doing and takes the first step. Good for communication, media, government dealings. Father is self-respecting and influential. With combust Mercury, speech carries authority but can sound commanding.

सिंह (मूलत्रिकोण) में तीसरे भाव में सूर्य: दृढ़ इच्छा-शक्ति, पहल करने वाले; पिता स्वाभिमानी व प्रभावशाली; वाणी में अधिकार।

Body areas to watch

heat, eyes, BP, headaches

Moon चंद्र 2nd lord, exalted and vargottama in the 12th

Exalted, vargottama Moon in Rohini in the 12th. Calm, kind, artistic, attached to comfort and beauty. Feelings stay private. Strong pull toward foreign places, retreats and spiritual rest. Money (Moon is 2nd lord) leaks into comfort and travel. Mother is caring and protective, possibly living at a distance later.

रोहिणी नक्षत्र में उच्च वर्गोत्तम चंद्र, 12वें भाव में: शांत, कलाप्रिय, सुख-सौंदर्य प्रिय; भावनाएँ निजी; विदेश व आध्यात्म की ओर झुकाव; माता स्नेही।

Body areas to watch

sleep, cold/cough, water retention, left eye

Mars मंगल 6th and 11th lord in the 6th, own sign (Harsha yoga); Darakaraka

Mars in its own sign Scorpio in the 6th: Harsha yoga. Fights and wins; strong immunity; good for jobs with competition, health, engineering, finance or law. Aspects the Lagna (drive, directness), the Moon (quick temper) and the 9th (argues with authority on principle). As Darakaraka it shows an energetic, decisive spouse.

स्वराशि वृश्चिक में 6वें भाव में मंगल (हर्ष योग): संघर्ष में विजय, मज़बूत रोग-प्रतिरोध; जीवनसाथी ऊर्जावान व निर्णायक।

Body areas to watch

fever, cuts, inflammation, blood, acidity

Mercury बुध Lagna and 4th lord in the 3rd, combust

Lagna lord Mercury in Leo in the 3rd, combust and in Magha. Sharp, quick, good with words, numbers and skills; but self-doubt or nervous strain when the Sun (authority, father, boss) overshadows. Best results come from own effort and communication work.

लग्नेश बुध सिंह में 3रे भाव में, अस्त: तेज़ बुद्धि, शब्द व अंकों में कुशल; अधिकारी के सामने आत्म-संदेह या तनाव।

Body areas to watch

nerves, skin, anxiety, shoulders

Jupiter गुरु 7th and 10th lord in the 11th, retrograde, with Saturn (Raja yoga)

Jupiter, 7th and 10th lord, retrograde in Aries in the 11th with Saturn. Marriage and career both connect to gains and social networks. Aspects the 3rd (Sun, Mercury), the 5th (children) and the 7th (its own house) — the main protector of marriage and children in this chart. D9 and D10 both place it exalted.

सप्तमेश व दशमेश गुरु, 11वें भाव में शनि के साथ वक्री: विवाह व करियर दोनों लाभ से जुड़े; 5वें व 7वें भाव की रक्षा; D9 व D10 में उच्च।

Body areas to watch

liver, weight, sugar

Venus शुक्र 5th and 12th lord in the 2nd with Rahu, retrograde; Atmakaraka

Venus, 5th and 12th lord and Atmakaraka, retrograde in Cancer (Ashlesha) in the 2nd with Rahu. Strong desire for beauty, luxury and love; attractive speech; income through creative, luxury, foreign or tech channels. Relationships carry intensity and unconventional choices. Moon–Venus exchange ties family money to spending.

पंचमेश व द्वादशेश, आत्मकारक शुक्र, 2रे भाव में राहु के साथ वक्री: सौंदर्य, विलास व प्रेम की तीव्र इच्छा; रचनात्मक/विदेश से आय; संबंधों में असामान्य चुनाव।

Body areas to watch

throat, hormones, urinary system, sugar

Saturn शनि 8th and 9th lord in the 11th, debilitated, retrograde, Neecha Bhanga

Saturn, 8th and 9th lord, debilitated and retrograde in Aries in the 11th, with Neecha Bhanga and exalted in D9. Luck comes late and through persistence; early struggle in father's or family fortune turns into steady gains after 30. Saturn return (2028–30) is a turning point.

अष्टमेश व नवमेश शनि, 11वें भाव में नीच व वक्री, नीचभंग व D9 में उच्च: भाग्य देर से और परिश्रम से; 30 के बाद स्थिर लाभ।

Body areas to watch

joints, fatigue, legs, teeth

Rahu राहु in the 2nd (Cancer, Ashlesha) with Venus; works through Moon (12th) and Mercury

Rahu in Cancer in the 2nd with Venus. Unusual tastes in food and speech; earning through technology, foreign clients or unconventional fields; family values questioned at times. Rahu MD (2012–30) is the period of ambition, foreign exposure and fast change.

राहु कर्क में 2रे भाव में शुक्र के साथ: खान-पान व वाणी में असामान्यता; तकनीक/विदेश से आय; राहु महादशा (2012–30) महत्वाकांक्षा व तेज़ बदलाव का समय।

Body areas to watch

throat, teeth, allergies, diet

Ketu केतु in the 8th (Capricorn, Shravana); works through Saturn and Moon

Ketu in Capricorn in the 8th (Shravana). Intuitive, interested in research, astrology or spirituality; sudden changes feel familiar. Watch hidden or recurring health issues and in-laws relations; Ketu periods bring breaks and fresh starts.

केतु मकर में 8वें भाव में: अंतर्ज्ञान, शोध व आध्यात्म में रुचि; अचानक परिवर्तन; छिपे स्वास्थ्य विषयों पर ध्यान।

Body areas to watch

joints, back, unexplained symptoms
