Nature & personalityस्वभाव व व्यक्तित्व

Indicators · संकेत
  • Gemini Lagna; Lagna lord Mercury in the 3rd with a moolatrikona Sun
  • Mars (own sign) aspects the Lagna from the 6th
  • Exalted, vargottama Moon in Rohini in the 12th
  • Atmakaraka Venus with Rahu
Reading · फलादेश

Quick-minded, articulate and self-driven; prefers to do things their own way. Competitive and direct, sometimes blunt. Emotionally steady and private; needs rest, comfort and beauty around them. Strong artistic taste.

तेज़ बुद्धि, स्पष्ट वाणी, आत्मनिर्भर; प्रतिस्पर्धी व सीधी बात करने वाले। भावनात्मक रूप से स्थिर पर निजी स्वभाव; सौंदर्य व सुविधा प्रिय, कलात्मक रुचि।

Educationशिक्षा

Indicators · संकेत
  • 4th lord Mercury in the 3rd (communication, commerce, computing), combust
  • 5th lord Venus with Rahu in the 2nd
  • Jupiter and Saturn both aspect the 5th
  • Rahu–Saturn AD (2017–20) overlapped Ashtama Shani (2017–20)
Reading · फलादेश

Suited to commerce, accounts, IT, languages and design. The graduation years 2017–2020 show pressure, a change of course or a delay. Check this against real dates first; it is the best test of this chart.

वाणिज्य, लेखा, आईटी, भाषा व डिज़ाइन के लिए अनुकूल। 2017–2020 में पढ़ाई में दबाव, विषय-परिवर्तन या देरी — पहले इसे वास्तविक घटना से मिलाएँ।

Careerकरियर

Indicators · संकेत
  • 10th lord Jupiter in the 11th with 9th lord Saturn (Raja Yoga)
  • Mars in its own sign in the 6th (Harsha yoga): service and competition
  • D10: Jupiter exalted in Cancer
  • Jupiter Mahadasha (10th lord) runs 2030–2046
Reading · फलादेश

A salaried or service career in a structured organisation suits best: finance, banking, teaching or advisory, IT/data, healthcare or administration. Growth is steady rather than sudden. Strongest period: Jupiter Mahadasha from Jul 2030. Rahu–Sun AD (2027) favours a push for recognition or a government link.

संगठित संस्था में सेवा-आधारित करियर उत्तम: वित्त, बैंकिंग, शिक्षण/परामर्श, आईटी, स्वास्थ्य या प्रशासन। प्रगति स्थिर; सबसे मज़बूत समय गुरु महादशा (जुलाई 2030 से)।

Wealthधन

Indicators · संकेत
  • Jupiter + Saturn in the 11th: strong, slow gains
  • Venus + Rahu in the 2nd: unconventional or foreign-linked income
  • 2nd lord Moon exalted in the 12th, in exchange with Venus
Reading · फलादेश

Earning capacity is good and grows with age. Money also goes out on comfort, travel and family. Savings need a system; a strong year can still end with less in hand than expected.

कमाई अच्छी व उम्र के साथ बढ़ती। सुख-सुविधा, यात्रा व परिवार पर खर्च भी; बचत के लिए नियम ज़रूरी।

Marriageविवाह

Indicators · संकेत
  • 7th house Sagittarius is empty and aspected by its own lord Jupiter (protective)
  • 7th lord Jupiter in the 11th with Saturn: marriage through a social or friend circle; a mature partner; some delay
  • Venus (karaka) retrograde with Rahu: unconventional choice, possibly a different community or region
  • Darakaraka Mars in its own sign; D9 Lagna Taurus holds the Moon
  • Manglik from the Moon only, cancelled
Reading · फलादेश

Marriage is stable once it happens; Jupiter guarding the 7th protects it. Expect a partner who is practical, mature or older, possibly from a different background. Double transit on the 7th (Jupiter + Saturn both aspecting Sagittarius): 1 Nov 2026 → 26 Jan 2027 and 21 Oct → 27 Nov 2027. The current Rahu–Venus AD (to 27 Jan 2027) supports the first window.

विवाह के बाद स्थिरता; 7वें भाव पर गुरु की दृष्टि रक्षा करती है। जीवनसाथी व्यावहारिक, परिपक्व या उम्र में बड़ा, संभवतः अलग पृष्ठभूमि से। द्विग्रह गोचर: 1 नवंबर 2026 → 26 जनवरी 2027 तथा 21 अक्टूबर → 27 नवंबर 2027। राहु–शुक्र अंतर्दशा (27 जनवरी 2027 तक) पहली खिड़की को बल देती है।

Childrenसंतान

Indicators · संकेत
  • 5th Libra; lord Venus retrograde with Rahu in the 2nd
  • Jupiter (putrakaraka) aspects the 5th: protective
  • Saturn also aspects the 5th: delay or a planned gap
Reading · फलादेश

Children are indicated, likely after some delay or by planning. Supportive periods: Rahu–Moon AD (Dec 2027 – Jun 2029) and Jupiter Mahadasha (from 2030).

संतान योग है, कुछ विलंब या योजना के बाद। सहायक समय: राहु–चंद्र अंतर्दशा (दिसंबर 2027 – जून 2029) और गुरु महादशा (2030 से)।

Healthस्वास्थ्य

Indicators · संकेत
  • Mars in its own sign in the 6th: strong immunity and recovery
  • Lagna lord Mercury combust: nervous strain, overthinking
  • Moon in the 12th: sleep quality matters
  • Ketu in the 8th: hard-to-diagnose or recurring complaints; joints and lower back
Reading · फलादेश

Constitution is good and recovery is fast. Watch stress and sleep, throat, skin and digestion (Venus–Rahu in the 2nd affects diet), and joints later. Sade Sati peak (2030–32) is the time to keep routines tight.

शरीर-बल अच्छा, रोग से जल्दी उबरना। तनाव, नींद, गला, त्वचा, पाचन पर ध्यान; साढ़ेसाती का चरम (2030–32) सावधानी का समय।

Foreign & travelविदेश व यात्रा

Indicators · संकेत
  • Rahu Mahadasha running (2012–2030)
  • 12th lord Venus with Rahu
  • Exalted Moon in the 12th
  • Rahu–Moon AD Dec 2027 – Jun 2029
Reading · फलादेश

Foreign connections are clearly indicated: MNC work, foreign clients or travel. Relocation is most likely in Rahu–Moon AD (Dec 2027 – Jun 2029), which overlaps the Sade Sati rising phase (Saturn 12th from the Moon).

विदेश से जुड़ाव स्पष्ट: बहुराष्ट्रीय कंपनी, विदेशी ग्राहक या यात्रा। स्थान-परिवर्तन की सबसे अधिक संभावना राहु–चंद्र अंतर्दशा (दिसंबर 2027 – जून 2029) में।
