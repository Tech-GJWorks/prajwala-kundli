Family — Parents, Siblings, Children, In-lawsपरिवार

Fatherपिता

Chart: 9th Aquarius, lord Saturn debilitated in the 11th with Jupiter; Sun (karaka) moolatrikona in the 3rd

Self-made, disciplined, respected; faced struggle or delays in the native's early years and rose later. Health care for father in Rahu–Saturn (2017–20, past) and Jupiter–Saturn (2032–35).

स्व-निर्मित, अनुशासित, सम्मानित; आरंभ में संघर्ष, बाद में उन्नति। 2017–20 व 2032–35 में पिता के स्वास्थ्य पर ध्यान।

Motherमाता

Chart: 4th Virgo, lord Mercury combust in the 3rd; Moon (karaka) exalted in the 12th

Caring, intelligent, sacrificing; may live at a distance later or travel often. Stress for mother in Kantaka Shani (2006–09) and Sade Sati (2027–34).

स्नेही, बुद्धिमान, त्यागमयी; बाद में दूरी संभव। 2006–09 व 2027–34 में माता पर तनाव।

Siblingsभाई-बहन

Younger sibling capable and outspoken; elder sibling or elder cousins responsible and helpful. Gains through siblings' networks.

छोटे भाई/बहन सक्षम; बड़े भाई/बहन ज़िम्मेदार व सहायक।

Children indicated, possibly after a planned gap. Windows: Dec 2028 – Mar 2029, Oct 2029 – Jan 2030 (double transit on the 5th), Rahu–Moon AD, and Jupiter MD 2030–36. Likely one or two; first child brings fortune.

संतान योग; कुछ अंतराल के बाद। समय: दिसंबर 2028 – मार्च 2029, अक्टूबर 2029 – जनवरी 2030, गुरु महादशा 2030–36। एक या दो, पहली संतान भाग्यशाली।
